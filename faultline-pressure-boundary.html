Why here
The body is Inconel 718 carrying 50 bar MEOP while cycling between ~290 K and 90 K over 10 reuse missions — a combination that drives thermal-mechanical fatigue at welds and section transitions. Because the fluid is LOX, any breach releases a strong oxidiser into the aft compartment rather than an inert fluid. The rationale fails if the fluid were inert, the duty single-use, or the pressure low.
Effect
Through-wall crack or rupture of the body or weld; LOX and GOX escape the boundary at up to 50 bar. → High-rate LOX release into the stage-2 aft compartment; loss of feed-line pressure to the turbopump; oxygen-enriched environment around hot engine hardware. → Uncontained LOX release, credible immediate fire during ascent — loss of vehicle.
Causes
Weld defect (lack of fusion, porosity) in the body closure or flange weld acting as a crack initiator, propagated by combined 0–50 bar pressure cycles and 90–290 K thermal cycles over 10 missions — a subcritical flaw that passes initial proof can grow to critical size between flights. | Low-cycle fatigue at the flange-to-body transition from repeated chilldown thermal shock — a classic cryo-valve crack driver at thick-to-thin transitions.
Controls
Prevent: qual to 4× life incl. pressure and thermal cyclings · material certs and heat-treat verification, IN718 bodya · weld NDI at manufacture and each refurbishmenta · controlled chilldown ratea.  Detect: proof-pressure test and helium leak check at acceptance and each refurbishmenta · feed-line / tank pressure telemetry, downstream effect onlya.

D 8 — operational anomaly evident only via downstream effects. Raised from 7 by self-review (note 1 below). Crack initiation is catchable only by NDI and proof at refurbishment, both assumed controls; final fracture in flight has effectively no detection before the consequence, and a ground-only check cannot claim a lower D for a mode whose onset occurs between inspections in reuse.
Recommended action  Establish a fracture-control plan for the body: fracture-mechanics life analysis against an NDI-detectable initial flaw size, mandatory weld NDI plus proof and helium leak check at every refurbishment, and chilldown rate limits.
